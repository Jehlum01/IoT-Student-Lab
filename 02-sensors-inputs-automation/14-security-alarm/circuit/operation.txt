Operating Logic
Motion detected
      ↓
Alarm activated
      ↓
LED ON + Buzzer ON
      ↓
5 seconds
      ↓
LED OFF + Buzzer OFF
      ↓
System armed again
Testing
Upload the program.
Open Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
Allow the PIR sensor to stabilize.
Move in front of the sensor.
Verify that the LED and buzzer activate.
Wait for the alarm timeout.
Verify that both outputs turn OFF.
Trigger the sensor again.
Troubleshooting
Alarm never activates

Check:

PIR VCC
PIR GND
PIR OUT → D2
LED wiring
Buzzer wiring
PIR stabilization time
Alarm activates repeatedly

Possible causes:

Continued movement in the PIR detection area
PIR sensitivity too high
PIR module time-delay configuration
Sensor positioned toward a heat source
Buzzer does not sound

Check:

Buzzer polarity
Buzzer type
D9 connection
Ground connection

If using a larger buzzer, use a suitable transistor driver.

Safety

This is a low-voltage educational alarm.

Do not connect mains-powered sirens, lamps, or other high-voltage loads directly to Arduino GPIO pins.

Use appropriate isolated switching hardware for real installations.
